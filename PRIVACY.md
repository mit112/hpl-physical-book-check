# Privacy policy — HPL Physical Book Check

Effective date: October 13, 2026. Publisher: **Mit**.

## Plugin operation

This skills-only plugin provides instructions and an optional local Python evidence processor. Mit operates no plugin server, telemetry, analytics, advertising, payment system or account database. The package does not send chat content, searches, reading lists or library-account information to a publisher-operated service. The optional processor handles supplied evidence locally and can write local evidence and report files where the operator runs it.

Your AI host receives your prompts and may access the public HPL catalog through its own permitted tools. The host, HPL, GitHub and any other service you choose operate independently under their own policies. Their services may collect request or account data. This policy does not replace those services' policies. The plugin does not require library sign-in or authorize sharing credentials.

## Support information

Mit receives information you choose to submit through repository issues or other direct support contact. GitHub issues are public: issue text, account identifiers and attachments may be visible to anyone. Submit only information needed to explain the problem, and redact sensitive details.

Mit uses support information to respond to requests, investigate problems and maintain the plugin. Public issue information is visible through GitHub and may be copied by others. Do not put private chat transcripts, library-card numbers, credentials or personal reading history in an issue.

Support information remains in the issue or correspondence history unless removed. There is no promised automatic deletion schedule. You may edit or delete your own issue content where GitHub permits, or request removal through a repository issue without reposting sensitive information. Mit can act only on information under Mit's control; copies held by GitHub, the AI host, HPL or other people are governed by those parties.

## Contact and changes

For questions about this policy or publisher-held support information, use this repository's Issues tab. Changes to this policy will be committed here with an updated effective date.
