# Answers people can use

The opening should answer the user's actual question without requiring them to read a research report. Keep supporting evidence available below it. These layers change presentation, not the evidence needed for a verdict.

## Gather useful follow-up facts

Do this quietly during the current read-only lookup, before answering. First finish the requested check or reach its permitted stopping point. Reuse inspected item rows and branch information; additional research should answer a likely next question about the same work or location.

| What the user may need next | Useful evidence to collect |
| --- | --- |
| Another way to get the same book | Confirmed available copies at other named branches; regular versus large print, labeled separately |
| Whether to wait for the selected branch | Its observed checked-out status and due date; a due date is not a promised return or availability date |
| Planning a visit to a confirmed location | The official branch page's address, hours and relevant closure notices |
| Getting a copy to their branch | Official HPL hold/pickup guidance and any material-specific restrictions; general guidance does not prove a particular copy can transfer |

In thorough mode, use already collected evidence first, then inspect up to two additional relevant official HPL pages or already discovered matching catalog records if they would help the next decision. This limit applies only to optional enrichment; it does not shorten the requested thorough search. Choose the most useful facts rather than performing every row of this table. Honor explicit limits such as “only Heights,” a language or edition restriction, or “just the answer.” Keep other works and digital/audio formats outside the lookup unless requested.

In quick mode, stop at the qualifying positive and reuse only related facts already observed. When identity is ambiguous, ask the identifying question before holdings or enrichment. If catalog access is blocked, keep its recovery budget exhausted; an independent official branch/policy page may still be checked within the enrichment limit. A failed optional check leaves the primary verdict and completeness unchanged.

Retain each related finding's source URL, actual observation time and scope alongside the ledger. Use observed record links for copies and official HPL links for branch/policy facts. Apply the same identity, freshness and item-status rules as the primary check. Do not infer “nearest,” “open now,” lending eligibility or transfer eligibility from a branch name or an Available label. Work happens within this lookup; do not imply research will continue after the answer.

## 1. Direct answer

Start with one or two plain sentences, without a Summary heading. Name the requested book and branch, and answer whether a matching print copy is available. Include a shelf number or next action here when that is what the user asked for. If the only confirmed copy is reference/special-collection material, say upfront that the catalog marks it Available but borrowing eligibility is unverified.

| Reconciled result | Opening in everyday language |
| --- | --- |
| Available, complete | “Yes—*Title* is available in print at Heights.” |
| Available, partial or quick | “Yes—I confirmed one print copy of *Title* at Heights. This was a partial check; other editions haven't all been checked.” |
| Unable to verify | “I couldn't confirm whether *Title* is available at Heights. The catalog stopped loading before I could finish.” Use the actual cause. |
| Held, none available | “Heights has print copies of *Title*, but none are available right now.” |
| No branch copy shown | “The completed check found no Heights print copy of *Title* in the catalog.” |
| No matching print record found | “I couldn't find a matching print record for *Title* in HPL's catalog after completing the searches.” |

These are shapes, not fixed scripts. Preserve the distinction between a completed negative and an incomplete check. A confirmed alternative at another branch does not turn an unverified selected-branch answer into “No.” A partial positive's count is a confirmed minimum; its title-wide total is unknown. If the user's intended work is unresolved, the answer is the short candidate list and one identifying question, without empty report sections.

If the user asks for just the answer, return this opening and its observed source link, keeping material uncertainty visible. Retain the supporting detail in the ledger instead of adding report sections.

For several books, lead with the practical result, for example “Of your three books, *The Hobbit* is available at Heights.” Explicitly account for unverified or unchecked titles in that opening when present. Follow with a compact table: **Book | At Heights | Shelf number**. Substitute the selected branch or HPL. Include every unique requested work, with its author when needed to identify it. Put “partial check” next to any partial positive, and “couldn't verify” or “not checked” where appropriate. Use observed catalog links on titles. Keep raw status, edition evidence, alternatives and coverage out of this decision table; they belong below.

## 2. Details

Use a **Details** heading for supporting information the user can read without asking again. If the host provides a supported disclosure control, these details may be expandable; otherwise ordinary Markdown headings work. Keep them proportional to the request:

- The inspected editions, regular versus large print, confirmed available/held counts, shelf/call numbers and observed catalog links. Unknown totals stay unknown; do not merge unresolved cross-record duplicates.
- Raw unavailable statuses and due dates where relevant. Put any restriction on borrowing reference/special-collection material beside that finding.
- The actual language/print scope and America/Chicago check time or interval. State a scope assumption briefly. Use the actual observation time, not the time the answer was written.
- A compact coverage note: pages screened, matching print editions inspected, and any pending work and stopping cause. Unknown denominators stay unknown. For lists, give per-work gaps and edition detail in short bullets or a separate evidence table. Reconcile all works' exclusive status and completeness counts internally; show a natural-language count summary rather than five zero-filled buckets.

Keep the full query history, excluded records and item identifiers in the ledger for an audit request. The normal answer still discloses every gap that affects its verdict or totals. Close the details with a brief freshness reminder such as “Availability can change; check the catalog before going.” If discussing holds or pickup, state here that no holds were placed.

## 3. Also useful

When related findings help, add **Also useful** after Details with up to three short bullets. Give the actual verified facts and their links so the user benefits immediately, rather than a generic “Want me to check other branches?” Offer fewer bullets or omit the section when no useful evidence was obtained or the user requested only the direct answer.

Keep each fact attached to its book, edition and branch. Label non-exhaustive alternatives “another confirmed option” or “in the records checked”; do not imply all HPL options were checked unless they were. An ordinary branch alternative needs its observed status and location; add borrowing/transfer caveats when advising pickup or for restricted material. A reference or special-collection item must carry its borrowing/transfer uncertainty in the same bullet. Branch hours and policy findings need their own official source; never invent them to fill this section. An optional lookup failure needs mention only if it leaves a material next-step uncertainty, such as unverified pickup eligibility.

>
> **Details**
>
> - Heights, regular print: 1 available copy, shelf number **FIC TOLKIEN**; another copy is **Due 10/23/26**. Total across all print editions: unknown.
> - English print. Screened 1/1 result pages; inspected 1/2 matching print editions. Pending: large-print holdings, which stopped loading after permitted recovery.
> - Catalog link and check time: not supplied in this simulation. Availability can change; check the catalog before going.
>
> **Also useful**
>
> - Another confirmed option in the records checked: **Looscan**, 1 regular-print copy marked Available, shelf number **FIC TOLKIEN**.
